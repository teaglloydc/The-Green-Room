About Us.

The Green Room is a self-directed compassion-based program designed to support mothers after preterm birth.

Teagan Lloyd-Collins

PhD Candidate, The University of Queensland

Teagan Lloyd-Collins is a PhD candidate at the University of Queensland. Her research is focused on developing and evaluating online, compassion-based interventions to support parents after preterm birth.

Dr Grace C. Fitzallen

Lecturer, University of Tasmania

Grace C. Fitzallen is a Lecturer in the School of Psychological Sciences (Health) at the University of Tasmania. Grace's research is focused on the long-term follow-up of high-risk infants adopting an integrative, family systems approach, encompassing child neurodevelopment, parental self-relating, family adaptation, grandparent wellbeing, and global neonatal follow-up.

Associate Professor James N. Kirby

Associate Professor and Clinical Psychologist, The University of Queensland

James N. Kirby, Ph.D., is an Associate Professor, Clinical Psychologist, Director of the Clinical Programs, and Co-Director of the Compassionate Mind Research Group at the University of Queensland. His research is focused on compassion science and the effectiveness of compassion-based interventions.

James is also an Honorary Associate Professor of the Center for Compassion and Altruism Research and Education at Stanford University, an Honorary Member of the Compassionate Mind Foundation UK, a Fellow of the Mind and Life Institute, and a founding board member of the Global Compassion Coalition.
