What is The Green Room?

The Green Room is program grounded in Compassion Focused Therapy to support mothers who have experienced a preterm birth.

The program includes 8 self guided modules, each with a video, written information, exercises and an emotional check-in. Each module builds on the last.

Life with a preemie baby is busy. That's why our program is designed to fit around your schedule. You can work through it at your own pace and return whenever you need to. The aim is not perfection, but steady engagement.

How it works

Simple steps before you begin.

Step 1

Complete the survey

Read the information sheet, provide consent, and complete the pre-program measures via a secure Qualtrics survey.

Step 2

Receive your access link

Once your survey is submitted, you will automatically receive a secure access link by email.

Step 3

Begin the program

Click your link and begin working through the program modules at your own pace, in your own time.

Enter The Green Room

The Green Room proudly operates within Australia, supporting mothers across the country with their mental health and wellbeing goals.

The Green Room acknowledges Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples as the traditional owners of country throughout Australia, and their continuing connection to land, sea and community. We pay our respects to them and their cultures, and to elders both past and present.

The Green Room is not a replacement for therapy. If you need mental health support, please talk to your doctor.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000 for police and ambulance. If you are having suicidal thoughts or are feeling disorientated, it is important to get help immediately. For immediate support, call Lifeline on 13 11 14 (24 hours, 7 days a week).
