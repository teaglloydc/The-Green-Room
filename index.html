About the Program

Add text here.

The Green Room has been shaped to feel clear, warm, and easy to move through. Each module introduces one idea at a time, then gives you a practical way to pause, reflect, and practise compassion in daily life.

You can work through it slowly. You can come back to it when you need to. The aim is not to perform wellbeing perfectly, but to build a steadier relationship with your own mind.

How it works

Simple steps before you begin.

Step 1

Complete the survey

Read the information sheet, provide consent, and complete the pre-program measures via a secure Qualtrics survey.

Step 2

Receive your access link

Once your survey is submitted, you will automatically receive a secure access link by email.

Step 3

Begin the program

Click your link and begin working through the program modules at your own pace, in your own time.

The Program

Eight modules, one steady path.

Each module builds on the last, with space to listen, read, reflect, and notice what is happening in your emotional systems.
